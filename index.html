A scratch pad for raised beds plus cattle, goats, sheep, pigs, horses, or poultry. It is not a forecast, not your harvest, and not Agrivia customer results.

Example math only — not a savings claim or ROI from other farms.

Garden produce (per bed) $360
Livestock water and feed ($40 / head) $240
Drip vs hose ($12 / bed) $72

Formula: beds × a crop factor (garden $60, mixed homestead $50, berries $40, pasture $25) + livestock head × $40 + beds × $12 for drip. Rounded. Head count is cattle, goats, sheep, pigs, horses, or birds — pick what you keep. This is arithmetic, not a forecast for your place.
