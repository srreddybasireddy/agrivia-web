Example cost-savings calculator

A simple arithmetic worksheet. It is not your farm data, not a guarantee, and not based on Agrivia customer results.

Formula: acres × a crop factor (corn $25, soy $22, wheat $18, specialty $45), then split 55% / 45%. Cattle × $40. Rounded. Change the sliders to see the arithmetic, not a forecast for your operation.
