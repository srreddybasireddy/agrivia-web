Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Agrivia.ai (“Agrivia”, “we”) is the website at https://agrivia.ai. This policy covers that site. Separate iOS and Android apps are being built and are not publicly listed; they are not covered here until they ship.

1. Information we collect

If you email us, we receive whatever you send (name, address, message). The contact page opens your email app or Gmail; we do not store form submissions on this website.

  • Email you send us: used only to reply.
  • Cookie notice: we may store a local preference that you dismissed the notice.
  • Website chat: the browser stores an anonymous device ID in localStorage and sends your questions to our farm advisory API (api.agrivia.ai) through a Cloudflare Worker on agrivia.ai/api. We use that ID to keep a conversation thread. Do not send passwords or financial account numbers in chat. Chat does not require a Google account.
  • Optional Google sign-in: if you sign in, Google sends us an ID token. We store a Google account id, email, and a farm profile (name, phone, ZIP, address, animals and beds you add) on the Agrivia API. Signing in is optional. We do not receive your Google password.

We do not currently run Google Analytics or affiliate-network tracking pixels on this site.

2. How we use data

  • To answer support and partnership email.
  • To operate this website and the on-site advisor.
  • To meet legal requests if required.

We do not sell your contact details.

3. Hosting and processors

The static site is hosted on Amazon S3 and served over HTTPS by Cloudflare. Chat requests may be processed by our application servers and model providers used by the Agrivia API.

4. Advertising (Google AdSense)

We are applying to Google AdSense so this website can show ads. Google advertising cookies and related technologies apply when ads are served on agrivia.ai. We are not currently a publisher on Amazon Associates, Awin, FlexOffers, Admitad, Impact, or similar affiliate networks. See our affiliate disclosure.

As required for Google advertising partners, this policy includes the following:

  • Third party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to your users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings (also called My Ad Center). Alternatively, you can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

More from Google: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

5. Your rights

To ask what we hold or request deletion of email correspondence, write to support@agrivia.ai. You can clear chat identity by deleting site data for agrivia.ai in your browser.

We store a local preference if you dismiss this notice. If ads are shown, Google and other vendors may use cookies. See our Privacy Policy.
