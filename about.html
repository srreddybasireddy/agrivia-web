About Agrivia

We write equipment primers for people with raised beds and for people keeping cattle, goats, sheep, pigs, horses, or poultry on a small place. A farm operations app is in development and is not in the public app stores yet.

Who we are

Agrivia is a small team in the United States. This website is for people watering raised beds, keeping a few cattle or goats, running a flock, or managing a few acres — not a commercial research staff, a 500-head feedlot, or a dealer network. We do not claim field-trial rankings on this site.

What we are building

The planned app includes crop notes, livestock logs, weather-risk alerts, and the same advisor this site already uses. Those app features are not in a store listing today. Chat here uses the Agrivia API through a Cloudflare Worker.
