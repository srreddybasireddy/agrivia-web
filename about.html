About Agrivia

This website is the live publication: equipment primers for raised beds and for cattle, goats, sheep, pigs, horses, or poultry, plus an on-site advisor. Separate iOS and Android apps are being built and are not in the public stores yet.

Who we are

Agrivia is a small team in the United States. This website is for people watering raised beds, keeping a few cattle or goats, running a flock, or managing a few acres — not a commercial research staff, a 500-head feedlot, or a dealer network. We do not claim field-trial rankings on this site.

What we are building

Separate iOS and Android apps are planned with crop notes, livestock logs, weather-risk alerts, and the same advisor this site already uses. Those apps are not listed in the App Store or Google Play. Chat on this website uses the Agrivia API through a Cloudflare Worker.
