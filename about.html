About Agrivia

We publish farm equipment guides and are building a farm operations app. The app is not in the public app stores yet.

Who we are

Agrivia is operated from the United States. This website is the public home for our guides and product updates. We are a small team building software for growers and ranchers. We do not claim a large research staff or published field-trial results on this site.

What we are building

The planned app includes crop timelines, livestock logs, weather-risk alerts, and an agronomy assistant. Those features are in development. This website uses AWS S3 and Cloudflare for HTTPS. Chat on this site uses the same advisory API as the apps, through a Cloudflare Worker.
