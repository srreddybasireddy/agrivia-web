Mobile home — keep the first screen to one job

Live phone today stacks a 3-line title, 2-line subtitle, a search that shares the row with a button, then two more full-width buttons. Search loses the row. Proposed: two-line title, one quiet line, one search bar, two buttons on one row.

  • Title stays the same words, just smaller so it fits two lines.
  • Subtitle shortens to one line. Badge stays, but smaller.
  • Search is one pill. The arrow lives inside it.
  • Ask advisor and Browse guides sit side by side. Equipment line is a caption, not another heading.
