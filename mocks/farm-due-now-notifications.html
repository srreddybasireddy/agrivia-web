Due now & notifications

Side-by-side mock of the Farm card and the header bell, on phone and desktop. Same rules on both sizes. No site code changed.

Proposed rules
  1. Due now — only overdue and due today, each line names the plant or animal.
  2. Coming up — optional short list of the next few future reminders (not in the bell).
  3. Bell — only alerts that need a look right now. Dot = unread. Done removes the alert.
  4. Labels use plain words: Water · Sugar cane, never pest_check · Oct 31….
